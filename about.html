About

Lore, origin, and the people behind it.

What is Femify?

Femify is a fresh rewrite of an older Spotify enhancer, retuned specifically for femtanyl: ALL THE TIME / KATAMARI / MAN BITES DOG (album) / LOVESICK, CANNIBAL! / PUSH UR T3MPRR. Where the original was general-purpose, Femify leans into distorted 808s, fast breaks, pitched-up vocals, and already-hot masters.

Why it exists

One day I was sitting in class listening to music. I saw that femtanyl released ALL THE TIME. Then I remembered about the old Spotify enhancer, and thought, "Maybe I should make one for femtanyl." Then, in just a few hours, Femify was made. An enhancer made for femtanyl/breakcore.

The name

femtanyl + ify = Femify.

Tech

  • Python 3.10+, PySide6 (Qt), sounddevice, NumPy, SciPy
  • WASAPI capture/playback at 48 kHz, 2 ch, 1024-block
  • RBJ biquad EQ, musical tanh soft-knee limiter
  • Packaged with PyInstaller as a single .exe
