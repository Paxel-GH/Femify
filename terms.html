Terms & Disclaimers

Read this before you turn it up.

Hearing damage warning. Femify can apply 1.5x–5.0x gain (+3.5 dB to +14 dB) on top of already-hot Femtanyl masters, plus up to ±32 dB of EQ in advanced mode. This can cause permanent hearing damage. We have warned you. It is not our fault if your ears hurt.

Hearing safety

  • Start at low gain (1.5x) and raise gradually.
  • Keep the soft limiter on unless you know what you're doing.
  • Take breaks. If it hurts, stop.
  • The advanced EQ (±32 dB) is gated behind a 5-second warning for a reason.

Windows-only

Femify targets Windows 10/11 using WASAPI. It requires VB-Audio Virtual Cable to route Spotify. Other platforms are not supported.
